    How to play

    Every day brings five estimation questions. You get one guess per question, and you can answer them in any order: tap a number above the card to jump around.

    Each guess is scored by how many times off it was. 1× is exact; 2× means you guessed double or half the answer. Lower is better. Your daily score is the average of the five.

    • 🎯 within 1.1×
    • 🟢 within 2×
    • 🟡 within 5×
    • 🟠 within 10×
    • 🔴 more than 10× off

    Type numbers any way you like: 1500, 1.5k, 2 million, 3B, 1e9, or a sum such as 365*24*60. A calculator lives in the top bar, with a notes box for your working that is saved with the puzzle.

    Unlimited keeps serving fresh questions for as long as you like. Timed gives you five minutes to answer as many as you can; an answer within 10× counts as in the ballpark.

    For distances, weights, areas, volumes, speeds and temperatures, the unit menu inside the box lets you answer in miles, pounds, °F and so on. The game converts your guess and remembers your choice.

    No looking things up: the fun is in reasoning your way there. A new puzzle arrives at midnight, your local time. Streaks count puzzles finished on their own day; the archive is just for fun.

    Your results are saved in this browser, and every finished puzzle is listed under Statistics, where you can also copy a backup code to carry your history to another device. On claude.ai they are saved to your account as well.

    After each answer you see how other players guessed. To make that work, each guess is sent to the game's server under a random id (or your username if you are signed in); no names or other details are collected.

    Every question is a back-of-the-envelope estimate. Is your answer in the ballpark?
